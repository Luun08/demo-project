# Existing-system selection and UX comparison criteria

**Owner:** Zhuparbai Yerkebulan — Frontend and UX  
**Task:** SSP-34  
**Status:** Desk-review scope; product table and sources are in [existing-system-comparison.md](existing-systems-comparison.md).

## Selected systems

The comparison uses four adjacent products: Birdhouse for Autism, Autism Tracker Pro, CareClinic, and MyChart Proxy. The set covers a family autism journal, a configurable autism-tracking app, a general care tracker with sharing, and a health-system portal with proxy access. These products are not all direct competitors; they were selected to compare workflows that overlap with family record keeping, observations, appointments, sharing and proxy access.

## User-facing comparison criteria

| ID | Criterion | Question for the comparison |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | SEN-specific focus | Is the product designed for a child/SEN context or for general health use? |
| C2 | Developmental/behaviour journal | Can a caregiver record observations over time? |
| C3 | Sessions/appointments | Does it help coordinate appointments, sessions or reminders? |
| C4 | Caregiver-specialist sharing | Can families share information with caregivers or professionals? |
| C7 | Export/reporting | Can users view summaries or export/share records? |
| C8 | Web/cross-device access | Which platforms or devices are documented? |

Security and privacy criteria C5, C6, C9 and C10 are defined separately in [system-security-criteria.md](system-security-criteria.md). The evidence table applies the combined criteria consistently; product-specific evidence and limitations are recorded in [existing-system-comparison.md](existing-systems-comparison.md).

## Evidence rule

Use public product documentation, dated listings or help pages. Record the source, access date, product/version and region where available. Mark a capability “not established” when the reviewed source does not document it; do not infer absence from silence or from a general marketing claim.
