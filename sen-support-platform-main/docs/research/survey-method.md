# Survey method and evidence limits

An anonymous Google Forms survey was conducted on 22–23 October 2026. The available summary reports seven respondents: three parents/guardians and four specialists (two speech therapists, one teacher, and one tutor). Detailed response summaries and the mapping from findings to requirements are in [evidence-to-requirements.md](evidence-to-requirements.md).

The survey link was shared in WhatsApp groups, Telegram groups, and groups on other messaging platforms. The raw response export is not committed because one response contains personal contact information. Findings marked as assumptions in the evidence table remain assumptions.

The survey is the project's current user-research evidence. The separate [usability plan](../frontend/usability-plan.md) describes a future evaluation of the interactive prototype; usability results have not yet been collected.
